## Projects

### 01 — Peripheral Nerve Conduit

**Application:** Peripheral nerve regeneration
**Software:** SOLIDWORKS

Parametric PCL nerve conduit with internal spiral fibre architecture.

* Length: 30 mm
* Outer diameter: 4 mm
* Wall thickness: 0.7 mm
* Helix/spiral and sweep features
* Parametric and feature-based modelling

### 02 — Coronary Stent

**Application:** Vascular intervention
**Software:** SOLIDWORKS

Parametric coronary stent concept demonstrating patterned geometry and feature-based modelling.

* Parametric sketching
* Patterned geometry
* Dimensional constraints
* Implant-oriented design

### 03 — Mechatronics Robotic Arm

**Application:** Mechatronics and robotic system design

Mechanical robotic arm design incorporating component modelling, assembly, connector design, wiring, and engineering documentation.
