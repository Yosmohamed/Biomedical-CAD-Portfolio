# Biomedical CAD Portfolio

Biomedical engineering CAD portfolio focused on parametric SolidWorks design for medical devices, implants, and biomedical applications.

## About

This portfolio showcases biomedical engineering CAD projects developed using SolidWorks, with an emphasis on:

- Parametric CAD modelling
- Biomedical device design
- Implant design
- Design for additive manufacturing
- Feature-based modelling
- Engineering design documentation

## Projects

### 1. Peripheral Nerve Conduit

**Application:** Peripheral nerve regeneration  
**Software:** SolidWorks  
**Design:** Parametric PCL nerve conduit

A parametric nerve conduit designed for peripheral nerve regeneration, incorporating an internal spiral fibre architecture.

**Key design parameters:**

- Length: 30 mm
- Outer diameter: 4 mm
- Wall thickness: 0.7 mm
- Internal architecture: Spiral fibre
